ABOUT US

Consultancy in energy planning, regulatory aspects, and market studies

Acropolis Energia is a consultancy that combines over 45 years of experience in the Brazilian electricity sector with an updated strategic vision.

Founded by Reive Barros, former director of ANEEL, former president of EPE, and former Secretary of Energy Planning and Development at MME, our mission is to translate the complexity of the sector into concrete results. We operate with technical knowledge and a consolidated relationship network within the main agencies and companies in the electricity sector.
