DIRECTOR

Reive Barros

Electrical Engineer from the Polytechnic School of Pernambuco (Poli-UPE) and Master in Production Engineering from UFPE. Served as Secretary of Energy Planning and Development at MME, President of EPE, and Director of ANEEL, actively participating in the formulation of public policies and sector regulation.

Held technical and executive leadership positions in major companies, such as Engineering Director at CHESF and Systems Management Director at CELPE. Was a member of the board of directors and fiscal council of key institutions such as ONS, EPE, Norte Energia, and STN. Also has a strong academic presence as a Professor at Poli-UPE and Fundação Getúlio Vargas (FGV).
